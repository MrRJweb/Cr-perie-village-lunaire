What we do

Repairs Done Right

From everyday heels to heirloom restorations, every job leaves the bench hand-finished and guaranteed.

Sole & Heel Replacement

Full and half soles in leather or rubber, heels rebuilt to their factory shape. Walk out with shoes that feel new again.

Shoe Shine & Restoration

Deep clean, recolour and a patient hand polish. We bring tired leather back to a warm, honest glow.

Bag & Purse Repair

Straps, linings, clasps and edge work — careful repairs that respect the original stitching and hardware.

Zipper Replacement

Boots, bags and jackets fitted with heavy-duty zippers, finished so cleanly you'd never know it was replaced.

Leather Jacket Repair

Panel patching, seam and cuff work, new linings — plus conditioning that adds years of comfortable wear.

Key Cutting

House and padlock keys cut while you wait — accurate the first time, or we cut it again for free.

Our story

Six Decades of Honest Repair

The bench hasn't moved since 1962. What began as a one-man stall stitching soles and setting heels has been handed down through three generations — same tools, same standards, same stubborn belief that good leather deserves a second life.

We still do things the slow way: skiving by hand, stitching where others glue, and matching dyes by eye under the window light. It takes a little longer. It lasts a lot longer.
