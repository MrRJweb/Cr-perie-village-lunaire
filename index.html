The luthier's bench

Repairs & Setups, Done Right

Our in-house luthier has thirty years of sawdust under their nails. If it has strings and a soul, we can bring it back.

  • Setups

    from $65

    Action, intonation, neck relief, and a good talking-to. Leaves playing the way it should have from the factory.

  • Fret Work

    from $120

    Level, crown, and polish — through to partial and full refrets. Buzz-free, choke-free, gleaming.

  • Electronics

    from $45

    Scratchy pots, dead jacks, pickup swaps, shielding, full rewires. We chase the crackle so you don't have to.

  • Restringing

    from $15 + strings

    While-you-wait restrings with a fretboard clean and quick once-over. Browse the crates; we'll call your name.

  • Repairs

    quoted at the bench

    Cracks, breaks, lifting bridges, headstock heartbreak. Honest quotes before we touch a thing — heirlooms handled like heirlooms.
