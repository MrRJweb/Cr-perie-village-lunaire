# Auto & Bike — Repair Shop Website

A modern, single-page website for **Auto & Bike**, a full-service auto and bicycle
repair shop. *Keeping you rolling.*

## Highlights

- **Mechanical, confident design** — graphite & steel-grey palette with a racing-red
  accent and strong condensed headings (Barlow Condensed / Barlow).
- **Fully responsive** with smooth scrolling and a hamburger menu on mobile.
- **Self-contained** — one `index.html` with embedded CSS/JS and inline SVG
  placeholder imagery. No build step, no dependencies beyond Google Fonts.

## Sections

1. Sticky navbar (logo, Services, Book Service, Why Us, Contact)
2. Hero with workshop backdrop, dark overlay and CTA buttons
3. Services — Auto & Bike two-column card grid
4. Book a Service — front-end-only form with success message
5. Why Us — four trust badges
6. Gallery — six placeholder workshop photos
7. Testimonials — three review cards with stars
8. Hours & Location — opening hours beside a map placeholder
9. Footer with quick links and social icons

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any browser — that's it.

> All text, images, prices and contact details are placeholders; swap them for
> real content before going live. The booking form is front-end only.
